How Magic Chords works

1
Left hand picks the root
Hover a segment of the left radial and hold for a moment to lock a root note. Left hand alone plays a major chord.
2
Right hand shapes it
The right radial overrides the quality (minor, 7, sus...). In Melody mode, the right side becomes a playable note bar.
3
Chords sustain & glide
A held chord rings forever with smooth portamento between changes until you drop your hands.
